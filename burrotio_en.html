The Burro Tió Christmas experience

Christmas magic among miniature donkeys. 6, 7, 8 and 19 December, at 11:00 and 15:00, with limited places.

El Petit Refugi

Celebrate Christmas

Experience the magic of Christmas with the arrival of El Petit Refugi's very own Burro Tió, our donkey take on the Catalan Christmas log: a heart-warming tradition that respects the animals and loves being close to nature. Join us for an unforgettable morning or afternoon at our refuge with Kitty, Kika, Pepa, Nina, Foc, Leo and our whole miniature-donkey herd.

  • Meet our animals: get close to our lovable little donkeys in a unique setting.
  • The Camí Màgic: a special trail where the little elves guide us step by step to find the Burro Tió.
  • Songs, cuddles and magical moments: when we find it, we sing to it, stroke it and give it lots of love so it brings lovely gifts for everyone.
  • Duration: 2 hours.
  • 6, 7, 8 and 19 December, at 11:00 and 15:00 (limited places).
  • €28 per child. Accompanying adults go free.

Frequently asked questions

Before you come

Which days and at what time?

6, 7, 8 and 19 December, with two sessions each day: 11:00 and 15:00. The activity lasts 2 hours.

How much does it cost?

€28 per child. Accompanying adults go free, and so do under-2s.

Do we need to book?

Yes, places are limited. Book on WhatsApp at +34 648 911 106, by e-mail or through our booking page.

Can young children come?

Yes, children of any age are welcome with an adult. Under-2s go free.

What if it rains or we can't make it?

We'll find another date together at no extra cost. The booking isn't refunded, but your visit isn't lost.

Can we bring a pushchair or come in a wheelchair?

Yes. Access is wheelchair-friendly and you're welcome to bring a pushchair.

Where can we park?

You can park right next to the farm, on Carretera de Lourdes (C/ Subirans), Arenys de Munt.

Are there toilets?

The nearest toilets are in Parque de Lourdes, a 5-minute walk away.
