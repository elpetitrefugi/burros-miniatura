La Castanyera at El Petit Refugi

An autumn morning of chestnuts, nature and miniature donkeys. 31 October and 1 November, limited places.

El Petit Refugi

Welcome the autumn

Welcome autumn with the arrival of the real Castanyera of El Petit Refugi — the traditional Catalan chestnut seller, who respects animals and loves being out in nature. Join us for an unforgettable morning at the refuge in the company of Kitty, Kika, Pepa, Nina, Foc, Leo and the whole miniature donkey herd.

  • Walk through the natural surroundings with an autumn treasure hunt.
  • Interaction with our animals.
  • Includes the creative workshop “My little castanyera donkey”.
  • Roast chestnut and panellets tasting.
  • Duration: 2 hours.
  • 31 October and 1 November (limited places).
  • From €15 per person for bookings of 10 people.
  • Activity for children aged 3 and over.
